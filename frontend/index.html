Why TabPFN on Small Data?
Standard Gradient Boosting models (like XGBoost) require hundreds of samples to generalize non-linear traffic and weather patterns. TabPFN incorporates synthetic prior reasoning, yielding superior zero-shot and small-sample accuracy for home bakeries and tiffins that have only 50–200 historical orders!
